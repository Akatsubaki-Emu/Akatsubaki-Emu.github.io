About the catalogue

The updater checks configured public feeds, normalises app and version information, and removes duplicate bundle identifiers. Feed availability can change, and a configured feed is not necessarily live or compatible.

App files and signing are controlled by their respective publishers. Check an app's source and permissions before installing it.

Maintained by Xthrx · Updates are scheduled every six hours when the updater succeeds.
